\subsection{Calibration for the paired EEG design}
A further null experiment uses the 22-subject design matrix from the stage-2 Fpz--Cz paired analysis, including the intercept, night-order difference, and mean elapsed-time difference. Let $\widehat\beta$ and $\widehat e_i$ be the fitted coefficients and residual surfaces in that analysis. The simulated mean retains the two fitted nuisance terms and sets the intercept surface to zero. Independent subject error surfaces have covariance $\Sigma_{\mathrm{cal}}=(22-3)^{-1}\sum_i\widehat e_i\widehat e_i\trans$. We consider Gaussian errors and multivariate $t_5$ errors standardized to have this covariance. Both distributions are examined with a common scale and with age-dependent scales $\lambda_i\propto\exp(0.35z_i^{\mathrm{age}})$, normalized so that $22^{-1}\sum_i\lambda_i^2=1$, where $z_i^{\mathrm{age}}$ is age centered and scaled to empirical variance one. Scaling multiplies each error surface by $\lambda_i$. The grid and inference procedure match the paired analysis, with 1,000 replications and 399 bootstrap draws as in the other calibration experiments.

Table~\ref{tab:paired-calibration} reports rejection probabilities from 0.038 to 0.053 and simultaneous coverage from 0.947 to 0.962. These results support calibration for this design under the specified error distributions, with somewhat conservative rejection in the heavy-tailed and heterogeneous settings.

\subsection{Additional EEG results}
The 60-second windows contain 6,000 samples at the original sampling rate. Short-window sensitivity uses the first 3,000 samples from the same selected intervals. Average ranks are used for tied digital values. No amplitude rescaling is required for the rank spectra; ordinary log-power estimates use the calibrated EEG values. The smoothing operation is periodic on the full Fourier frequency interval before the positive-frequency analysis grid is extracted. Night-level weights in the age analysis are recalculated after channel-specific quality exclusions. The medication analysis averages the retained segment summaries within each night before taking paired differences.

Figure~\ref{fig:fullsurfaces} shows the full stage-2 coefficient surfaces. The age contrasts are expressed per decade, and paired contrasts are temazepam minus placebo. The black boundaries enclose grid cells where the corresponding within-surface simultaneous interval excludes zero; they are drawn between adjacent grid cells for visualization. The direct Pz--Oz age contrasts in Figure~\ref{fig:agecontrasts} compare the nonmedian quantile responses with the median response using their joint subject-level covariance. Table~\ref{tab:eeg-sensitivity} and Figure~\ref{fig:resolution} summarize sensitivity to window length and bandwidth.
