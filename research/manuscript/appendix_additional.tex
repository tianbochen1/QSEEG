\subsection{Calibration for the paired EEG design}
A further null experiment uses the 22-subject design matrix from the stage-2 Fpz--Cz paired analysis, including the intercept, night-order difference, and mean elapsed-time difference. Let $\widehat\beta$ be the fitted coefficient surface and let $\widehat e_i=(\widehat e_i(s_1),\ldots,\widehat e_i(s_D))\trans\in\R^D$ be the residual vector, where $D=540$. The simulated mean retains the two fitted nuisance terms and sets the intercept surface to zero. Before subject scaling, independent error vectors have covariance $\Sigma_{\mathrm{cal}}=(22-3)^{-1}\sum_i\widehat e_i\widehat e_i\trans$. This matrix has rank 19. The Gaussian and standardized multivariate $t_5$ errors thus vary within the residual subspace observed in the paired study. Both distributions are examined with a common scale and with age-dependent scales $\lambda_i\propto\exp(0.35z_i^{\mathrm{age}})$, normalized so that $22^{-1}\sum_i\lambda_i^2=1$, where $z_i^{\mathrm{age}}$ is age centered and scaled to empirical variance one. Scaling multiplies subject $i$'s error vector by $\lambda_i$, giving covariance $\lambda_i^2\Sigma_{\mathrm{cal}}$. The grid and inference procedure match the paired analysis, with 1,000 replications and 399 bootstrap draws as in the other calibration experiments.

Table~\ref{tab:paired-calibration} reports rejection probabilities from 0.038 to 0.053 and simultaneous coverage from 0.947 to 0.962. These results support calibration for this design under the specified error distributions, with somewhat conservative rejection in the heavy-tailed and heterogeneous settings.
